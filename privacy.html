Hedgerow Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2, 2026

Hedgerow is published by Cookbase LLC. It does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your game in progress, the player names you type, your win and loss record, and your settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

What we don't do

Hedgerow has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and it doesn't connect to any server of ours.

Tips

If you choose to leave a tip, Apple handles the payment through the App Store. We receive no payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to that purchase.

Children

Because Hedgerow collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. Questions are welcome on the support page.

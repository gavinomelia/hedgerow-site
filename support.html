Hedgerow Support

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub and tell us your device and iOS version.

How do I play?

Take a tile, place it touching the board, and optionally plant a flag on it to claim its patch. When all 30 tiles are down, each patch scores a point per tile for whoever has the most flags in it. Ties split the points. In the app, How to Play is on the home screen and in the ⋯ menu during a game.

Where did my game go?

Your game saves automatically on your device and reopens when you launch Hedgerow. Deleting the app deletes the saved game.

Do I have to pay for anything?

No. Hedgerow is free, with no ads. Tips are optional and unlock nothing.
